## Suggested review path

1. Sign in with the parent demo account. Email/password is the only primary login method; Sign in with Apple is not required for this authentication set.
2. Parent Mode opens the Home tabs. Open **My Teams**, select the sample team, open an announcement, submit a reply, and view the linked fictional child profile.
3. Open **Friends** and the prepared conversation. A message can be reported from its options; the user can be reported or blocked from conversation management. Blocked users are managed under **Profile → Account, privacy & support → Blocked users**.
4. Team announcements, replies, and private parent/coach messages also expose Report Content actions. Reports are stored for operator review; authorized coaches can remove team replies and announcements.
5. Open **Squads** and choose nearby discovery. Foreground location is requested only after this action and is used to find venue-and-sport communities; precise user coordinates are not shown publicly. Denying access leaves manual discovery available.
6. Open a team voice-message composer and deliberately start recording. Microphone permission is requested only then. Denial is handled with an explanation and Settings fallback.
7. Notifications are optional and enabled from Settings. They cover team, friend, and Squad updates and use privacy-minimal lock-screen copy.
8. Switch to Coach Mode from Profile. Open the sample team, roster, announcements, and coaching resources. Switch back to Parent Mode from the coach interface.
9. Open Games and test Bomb Defusal, Trivia Blitz, and Spot the Difference. Multiplayer requires the prepared second account/device; solo paths remain available where shown.
10. Complete the Weekly Challenge and view the Squad leaderboard.
11. Account deletion is at **Profile → Account, privacy & support → Delete account**. It requires the current password, typing `DELETE`, and a final destructive confirmation. **Do not delete the shared review account unless the owner has provided a disposable deletion-test account.**

## Safety and audience notes

Sideline Social is intended for adults involved in youth sports and is not submitted to the Kids Category. Parent-created child profiles are private and team-scoped. User-generated content is subject to authenticated authorization, reporting, blocking, coach moderation, limited severe-content screening, and documented operator review procedures.
