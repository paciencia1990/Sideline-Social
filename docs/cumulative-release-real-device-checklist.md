# Cumulative candidate real-device acceptance checklist

Candidate: `release/cumulative-rc-20260916` based on `df8af744538b034306e8eca71bb861a87a478c62` and bound to the candidate manifest.

Run this checklist on staging only. Complete the ordinary app path before activating or starting any timed moderation workflow.

## Build and install preflight

- Reconcile the exact candidate manifest and commit created from it.
- Confirm the build resolves to `sideline-social-staging-2026`; emulator mode is off.
- Confirm the Android application ID is `com.sidelinesquad.app.dev`, the visible name is `Sideline Social Staging`, and Google sign-in is enabled.
- Confirm the registered Android Firebase app, OAuth client and signing SHA fingerprints match the actual build credential.
- Obtain the authoritative next version code from EAS/Play history before building.
- Build one internal Android APK from the staging-acceptance profile and verify artifact provenance before install.
- Install alongside the everyday production app. Do not uninstall it, clear its data, or replace signing credentials.
- iOS currently shares the production bundle identifier. Use a separate device or separately approve/register a staging identifier before attempting a co-installable iOS build.

## Ordinary app journey

1. Launch cold and confirm the staging identity/configuration without exposing configuration values.
2. Sign into an existing approved staging account with email/password. Confirm the correct saved name, profile, and Home state.
3. Kill and relaunch the app. Confirm the session and profile persist, then sign out normally.
4. Confirm Google sign-in reaches the staging account flow and returns to the app. Do not proceed if the native OAuth client/package/signature is wrong.
5. Using an approved fresh synthetic account, complete normal account creation, legal/adult onboarding, name, sport and ZIP fields. Confirm its private profile and server-created public projection through the normal app/backend path.
6. Relaunch and confirm the new account remains signed in and retains its name/onboarding state.
7. With the two approved non-moderator accounts, search by the exact public name, send one friend request, accept it in the other session, and confirm both Friends views.
8. Open a direct conversation, exchange a text message in each direction, leave/reopen the conversation, and confirm authorization and persistence. Exercise media only if separately approved test content is available.
9. Confirm profile/header names in both accounts. Open My Teams and verify the expected memberships, selected team and role-specific controls. Treat missing membership as a prerequisite failure, not an app-profile workaround.
10. Exercise Coach AI keyboard behavior, result navigation and save/open guide flow under the approved staging provider configuration.
11. Exercise the changed games: synchronized lobby start, Trivia answer feedback, and Bomb Defusal replay/start flow. Confirm Bomb remains text-only and rejected autogenerated images are absent.

If any ordinary step fails, capture the exact app stage and sanitized error, preserve the account/data state, and stop before moderation activation.

## Moderation journey, only after the ordinary path passes

- Reconcile the exact deployed Mobile and Safety code/configuration with the accepted contracts; confirm all gates begin dark.
- Reuse only current, valid participant/staff receipts and establish observer/restoration readiness.
- Arm independent mandatory restoration before activation.
- Submit one approved synthetic complaint through the staging phone app and establish report persistence, case association, and the application-generated notification path.
- In the Console, verify the minimal claimable projection, explicit version-checked claim, post-claim detail access, requester action, lead approval, enforcement, evidence and audit entries.
- Verify the affected account receives the intended standing result through the real app path.
- Submit one actual affected-user appeal through the phone app; verify linkage, lead resolution, reversal and standing recovery.
- Distinguish provider acceptance from owner-confirmed inbox delivery for each application-generated email.
- Perform ownership-checked cleanup of only the fresh contract resources.
- Complete mandatory restoration even if cleanup fails, then independently verify reporting, enforcement and email gates are dark with the same accepted code.

Passing this checklist proves only the bounded staging candidate path. It is not a production rollout or store-submission approval.
